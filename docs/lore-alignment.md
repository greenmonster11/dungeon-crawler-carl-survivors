# Dungeon Crawler Carl Lore Alignment

This repo is an unofficial fan game inspired by the Dungeon Crawler Carl universe.

## Canon-aware goals

- Keep tone darkly comic, brutal, and satirical.
- Emphasize the "live show" framing: ratings, betting, sponsorships, audience manipulation.
- Keep Borant/corporate language cold, transactional, and entertainment-first.
- Keep crawler messaging survival-focused and desperate, not heroic fantasy.

## Writing voice rules

- System AI voice:
  - clinical, mocking, and ratings-obsessed
  - short broadcast-style lines
  - avoid repetitive single-gag fixation
- Viewer chat voice:
  - chaotic, memey, and adversarial
  - mix fans, haters, gamblers, and corporate accounts
- Boss/floor announcements:
  - frame every moment as an event for viewers
  - reinforce that danger is a product

## Content boundaries

- Avoid explicit sexualized fetish jokes in core system messaging.
- Avoid claiming uncertain fan-wiki details as hard canon.
- If uncertain on lore details, label new material as "DCC-inspired" instead of canonical.

## Gameplay alignment priorities

1. Show-pressure mechanics (ratings spikes, betting modifiers, sponsor objectives).
2. Crawl risk tradeoffs (survival vs spectacle rewards).
3. Character voice consistency (Carl, Donut, Mordecai-adjacent commentary tone).
4. Floor theming that feels like curated "show content" instead of random biomes.

## Pixel Art Generation Rules (Performance + Tone)

- Prefer pixel art only for runtime game assets (bosses, mobs, UI plates, pickups).
- Generate at small base sizes (`32x32`, `48x48`, `64x64`) and upscale with nearest-neighbor.
- Keep palettes tight (8-18 colors per asset) with strong value contrast and minimal alpha blending.
- Avoid painterly gradients, motion blur, and high-frequency detail that turns to visual noise on phones.
- Keep silhouettes readable at arm's length in portrait mode.

### DCC-inspired visual cues

- Arena TV framing: warning stripes, sponsor framing bars, broadcast overlays.
- Corporate cruelty: sterile labels, hazard colors, "productized violence" motifs.
- Brutal-comic vibe: exaggerated enemy shapes, high-contrast hit flashes, readable threat tells.

### Canon safety

- Use "DCC-inspired" for uncertain references.
- Avoid claiming fan-wiki-only details as hard canon.
